01 / The vehicle Two stages. Zero expended.

A rocket you fly,
not a rocket
you build.

The L-1 was drawn around one constraint: every piece that leaves the pad has to come back to it. That rules out the cheap answers — the expendable upper stage, the fairing written off at sea — and forces the expensive ones to get good.

Nine Meridian engines lift 682 tonnes off the mount. The booster turns around before it leaves the atmosphere and lands on the pad it departed. The second stage carries its own heat shield and enough margin to fly itself down an hour later.

05 / Mission profile Reference flight · 28.5 t to 420 km
  1. T−00:00:35

    Startup

    Nine engines spin up against the hold-down clamps while the flight computer reads the thrust curve.

  2. T+00:00:00

    Liftoff

    Clamps release at full thrust. The vehicle clears the tower in seven seconds.

  3. T+00:01:14

    Max Q

    Peak aerodynamic pressure. The centre engine throttles to 68%.

  4. T+00:02:38

    MECO

    Main engine cutoff. The booster has spent 71% of its propellant and kept the rest for the way home.

  5. T+00:02:43

    Stage separation

    Pneumatic pushers part the stages; the second stage ignites four seconds later.

  6. T+00:03:06

    Boostback burn

    Three engines reverse the booster's downrange velocity and aim it at Pad A.

  7. T+00:03:38

    Fairing separation

    Both halves split away and open parafoils for a guided descent to the recovery ship.

  8. T+00:06:24

    Entry burn

    A three-engine burn takes the heating load off the grid fins and the engine bay.

  9. T+00:08:02

    Booster landing

    Legs deploy at 400 m. The booster lands on Pad A, 240 m from where it lifted off.

  10. T+00:09:41

    SECO

    Second stage cutoff at 420 km, inclination 51.6°.

  11. T+00:14:00

    Payload deploy

    The bus separates and unfolds its arrays on the second orbital night.

  12. T+01:02:00

    Second stage return

    Deorbit burn, shielded re-entry and a propulsive landing at Pad C. Nothing is left in orbit.
