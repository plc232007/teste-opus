Act II — Manifesto

Everything worth building takes a decade.

I have never once been early. I have only ever been willing to wait longer than the room.

I grew up between a machine shop and a library, which is a decent education in the two things that matter: how a thing is actually made, and how long people have been wrong about it.

The companies I start share a shape. They pick a problem the market has priced as impossible, then spend seven unglamorous years making it boring. No pivot, no adjacency, no second act inside the first one.

I don't believe in vision statements. I believe in tolerances, thermal budgets, and the specific weight of a part you've held in your hand at four in the morning.
